# PDF Master

A free PDF editor for Mac, Windows and Linux. No subscription, and it works offline on your computer.

Read, edit text and images, comment, fill and sign forms, build forms, organize and combine pages, make scans searchable, password-protect, permanently redact, compress, convert and compare.

## Download

**[Get the latest version](https://github.com/zahidthani/PDF-Master-Downloads/releases/latest)**. You don't need a GitHub account. Scroll down to **Assets** and pick your computer:

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel), macOS 12 or newer | `PDF-Master-<version>-mac-universal.dmg` |
| Older Intel Mac, macOS 10.15 or 11 | `PDF-Master-<version>-mac-legacy-x64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11 | `PDF-Master-<version>-win.exe` (a smaller `-win-x64.exe` or `-win-arm64.exe` also works) |

## The first time you open it

The app isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate, so your computer warns you once.

- **Mac, macOS 15 or newer:** double-click the app and click **Done**. Then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Mac, macOS 14 or older:** right-click the app, then choose **Open** and **Open** again.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen appears, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** make the file executable with `chmod +x`, then run it.

Only download PDF Master from this page. To check that a download is intact, compare it with the `SHA256SUMS` files on the release page.

## How well it has been tested

This is an early test version. Before release, the real app was started automatically on Apple silicon and Intel Macs and on Windows, where it opened, edited and saved a file. Most hands-on use so far has been on one Intel Mac, so keep your own copy of important documents. Saving a redacted or sanitized document creates a new file, and your original stays as it was.

**Found a problem?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/zahidthani/PDF-Master-Downloads/issues). In the app, **Help › Report a Problem…** gives you details to paste in. Please don't attach private documents.
